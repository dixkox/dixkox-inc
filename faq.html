FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our services, the Academy, the community, the Festival and more.

General

What is DIXKOX Inc.?

DIXKOX Inc. is a registered Canadian technology and human-centred intelligent systems company. We build technology for organizations, train people for careers in tech, and grow a community, festival, labs and new ventures.

Where are you based?

We are based at 750 Wonderland Road South in London, Ontario, Canada, opposite Westmount Shopping Centre. We work with clients and learners in Canada and internationally, in person and online.

How do I contact you?

Call +1 (226) 386-4611, email info@dixkox.com or use the contact form. We reply to every message.

Technology services

What services do you offer?

IT consulting, software, website and web application development, AI and machine learning, data, cloud, Internet of Things, testing, cybersecurity and dedicated teams. See the Technology page for the full list.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is quoted after a free consultation, because cost depends on scope. You can choose a fixed-price project, a monthly retainer or a dedicated team.

How long does a project take?

A simple website can take a few weeks; larger applications take a few months. We agree a timeline with you before work starts.

Do you work with small businesses and startups?

Yes. We work with organizations of every size, from startups to established companies.

Can I see a demo first?

Yes. Demos and solution walkthroughs are free and take about 30 minutes. Request one from the Contact page.

Do you sign confidentiality agreements?

Yes. We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share project details.

Academy

When does enrolment open?

Enrolment is opening soon. Join the waitlist through the Contact page and we will tell you first.

Are the courses online?

Yes. Courses are delivered online with live and recorded lessons, so you can learn from anywhere.

Do I need experience to start?

No. Many courses start at beginner level. Each course lists the level it starts at and where it takes you.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. You receive a DIXKOX Academy certificate of completion when you finish a course.

Do you guarantee a job after the course?

No one can honestly guarantee a job. We prepare you with real projects, CV and interview support, and share opportunities from our network.

Community and Festival

What is the DIXKOX Tech Club?

A free club where people bring their ideas and innovations, get feedback from practitioners, team up with others and build prototypes. Strong projects can be showcased at the DIXKOX Festival and supported through DIXKOX Labs and Ventures.

Do I keep ownership of the idea I bring to the Tech Club?

Yes. Your idea stays yours. We do not share a submission outside the club without your permission. If a project becomes a partnership with DIXKOX, we agree the terms with you in writing first.

How do I join the community?

Membership is free. Email info@dixkox.com with your name and interests, and we will add you.

When is the DIXKOX Festival?

Dates and venue will be announced on the Festival page. Register interest to be told first.

How can my company sponsor or exhibit?

Email us with "Festival sponsorship" or "Festival exhibitor" in the subject and we will send the details.

Can I speak or volunteer at events?

Yes. Tell us what you would like to talk about or how you would like to help.

Ventures and partnerships

When will the Store, Marketplace and Payments open?

They are in development. Contact us to be notified when each one opens.

Can I donate to the DIXKOX Foundation?

Online donations are opening soon. To support the Foundation now, or to volunteer or partner, contact us.

How do I become a partner?

See the Partners page for partnership types, then email us about your organization and what you would like to do together.

Careers

Are you hiring?

We add roles as we grow. Send your CV to info@dixkox.com and we keep strong profiles on file.

Do you offer internships?

Yes. We offer internships and project placements for students and career changers.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly. We reply to every message.
